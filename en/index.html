PINEWOOD HOTEL DALAT

Pinewood Hotel Dalat

Pinewood Hotel Dalat is located at 54 Vo Truong Toan Street, Lam Vien - Da Lat. The hotel currently offers 50 rooms across eight room types, from Deluxe rooms to Suites, with clear information about room size, beds, views and rates for the published periods.

Guests can choose stay dates on the homepage to see an estimated room charge based on the current rate table. The estimate does not confirm availability; Pinewood will check availability and confirm the final rate after a booking request is sent.

Guests are supported with Wi-Fi in rooms and public areas, breakfast at the hotel restaurant from 06:30 to 09:00 and direct Reception assistance.
