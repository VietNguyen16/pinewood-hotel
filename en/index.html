PINEWOOD HOTEL DALAT

Pinewood Hotel Dalat

Pinewood Hotel Dalat is located at 54 Vo Truong Toan Street, Lam Vien - Da Lat. The hotel currently offers 50 rooms across eight room types, from Deluxe rooms to Suites, with clear information about room size, beds, views and amenities.

Guests can choose stay dates, room type and guest count on the homepage and then send a booking request. Pinewood will check and confirm room availability after receiving the request.

Guests are supported with Wi-Fi in rooms and public areas, breakfast at the hotel restaurant from 06:30 to 09:00 and direct Reception assistance.
