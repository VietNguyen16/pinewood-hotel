PINEWOOD HOTEL DALAT

A more responsible stay at Pinewood Hotel Dalat

Pinewood Hotel Dalat encourages guests to use resources responsibly during their stay in Da Lat. Simple choices such as reusing towels when appropriate, saving water, and switching off air conditioning and electrical equipment when leaving the room can reduce unnecessary waste while preserving a comfortable stay.

The hotel also aims to provide clear guest information so visitors can make informed choices about services and resource use throughout their stay.
